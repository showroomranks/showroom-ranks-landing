Getting started

Four steps to the leaderboard

No spreadsheets, no screenshots buried in a group chat. Just join and start showing off.

1

Join the server

Hop into the ShowRoom Ranks Discord — free, open to any MONOPOLY GO tycoon.

2

Set up your profile

Link your in-game name so your finds show up under the right name on the leaderboard.

3

Log your showroom

Submit your dice, shield, and token counts — by screenshot or command, whichever's faster.

4

Climb, hunt, repeat

Watch your rank move, jump into team scavenger hunts, and keep your showroom current every season.

Built by collectors, for collectors

ShowRoom Ranks started as one server's way of keeping score. It's grown into a proper collection tracker — a validated catalog, a live leaderboard, and a community that actually knows the game's scoring quirks better than any spreadsheet does.
